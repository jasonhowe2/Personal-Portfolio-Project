ABOUT ME

Curious about sports. Focused on growth.

I am a student at UNC Charlotte who enjoys sports, communication, and working with data. My interest in sports has led me to explore how statistics and analytics can explain changes in the game and support better decision-making from both sides of baseball. I also serve as Secretary for UNC Charlotte Club Baseball, giving me experience contributing to a team organization and communicating with players and others involved with the program.

Through coursework and projects like my MLB pitching and hitting analysis, I am developing skills in Python, pandas, data visualization, and exploratory data analysis. I enjoy taking a question that starts with curiosity, finding data that can help answer it, and turning the results into a story that someone else can understand.

My career goal is to continue building skills that allow me to work in sports, analytics, communications, or another role where data is involved.
